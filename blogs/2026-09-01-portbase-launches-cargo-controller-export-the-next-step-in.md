On May 18 2026, Portbase launched the new Cargo Controller Export (CCE) service. This service focuses on the export cargo flow, including vessel and terminal information. With this new service, we are addressing long-standing requests from the port community; for instance, the new service includes additional data points, (personalized) email notifications, and an API connection.
